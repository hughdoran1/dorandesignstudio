## Chronology

- c.1885–1895 | Birth, place unknown. Not found. Every "Cecil Stanley Price"
  hit was a namesake (see Namesakes). [U]
- 1914–1919 | AAMC service. Not found. No AIF Project, AWM, VWMA or NAA
  entry surfaced for "Cecil Stanley Price". The brief's "captain, AAMC" rests
  on the Cyril Kett museum catalogue only. [U]
- 1918–1920 | MBE. Not found in the search-indexed Wikipedia transcriptions of
  the 1918 New Year, 1918 Birthday, 1919 New Year, 1919 Birthday and 1920
  New Year MBE lists. Only Prices found: Lt William Price, 47th Bn AIF (1919
  New Year) and Gertrude Rangeley Stanley Price, Superintendent of Hostels,
  Ministry of Munitions (1919 New Year). Absence from an excerpt is not
  absence from the list. [S] The "Stanley Price" in Gertrude's name may be a
  compound surname and a family lead. (inf.)
- 1920s | Osteopathic training. Not found. Context only: British School of
  Osteopathy founded 1917, first two diplomas 1925 (Elsie Wynter Waring and
  Capt. Gerald Lowry, blinded in the war); Incorporated Association of
  Osteopaths founded 1925; General Council and Register of Osteopaths opened
  1939. [S] If his DO was British it can date no earlier than 1925; he is
  "Captain" not "DO" in Bates's magazine in 1925, and "M.B.E., D.O." by 1934.
  (inf.)
- by late 1924 | "There are now two clinics, and a Better Eyesight League in
  England, all reporting favorable results." Better Eyesight (New York),
  Jan 1925. [T]
- Jan 1925 | "Capt. C. S. Price, of London, England, plans to visit Dr. Bates
  around the latter part of January" to "discuss ... the best methods which
  are employed for the cure of imperfect sight without glasses"; "The spread
  of Dr. Bates' method in England is largely due to Capt. Price's enthusiasm
  and success in helping others"; expected at the February League meeting.
  Better Eyesight, Jan 1925. [T]
- Feb 1925 | "Clinic Reports From London": reports from Mr. Price of the
  "Better Eyesight League of Great Britain and Ireland"; the London clinic
  "accepted all cases", including those "with little perception of light".
  Better Eyesight, Feb 1925. [T]
- Feb 1925 (meeting), reported Mar 1925 | Speaker at the New York Better
  Eyesight League meeting: "Captain C. S. Price, of London". England had "a
  Better Eyesight League of many members" without regular meetings; members,
  "having no direct contact with Dr. Bates", learned the method "from an
  intensive study of the book". He spoke of "his own work and that of his
  colleague, Miss K. Beswick"; "back of all eye troubles are mental factors";
  he "has given much thought to the psychological side". "The work was first
  carried on in institutions, but as its unorthodox character became known,
  Captain Price and Miss Beswick were debarred from working there"; patients
  "followed them ... and visited them in their free time at the office".
  Cases cited: a man whose one eye had been removed and whose other eye had
  "not been used for eighteen years and had atrophied", able "to open the
  lids" after six weeks; a woman "blind eight and a half years from glaucoma"
  who "now could play cards without glasses". Better Eyesight, Mar 1925. [T]
- Spring 1925 | Bates's "thoroughly capable" endorsement after Price's stay
  as his guest. Not located in any excerpt. Most likely in the Apr, May or
  Jun 1925 issue (central-fixation.com better-eyesight-1925-04/-05/-06;
  cleareyesight-batesmethod.info id83 = Apr 1925, cleareyesight.info id85 =
  Jun 1925). [U]
- 1926–1930 | No further Price mention surfaced in excerpts of Better
  Eyesight (which ran to Jun 1930). Not searched issue by issue. [U]
- 1929 | Harry Benjamin, Better Sight Without Glasses (London: Health For
  All). No link to Price found. [S]
- 1934 | The Improvement of Sight by Natural Methods: a complete treatise
  upon the newer methods of treating all conditions of imperfect sight by
  natural means, and obviating the necessity for glasses, drugs, or
  operations, by C. S. Price. London: Chapman & Hall. First edition.
  Wellcome Collection work bt54e8sq (title as catalogued); Google Books id
  0lWuAAAAIAAJ; Open Library OL16163714M (author expanded as "Cecil Stanley
  Price"). [S]
- 1936 | Second edition, Chapman & Hall. Bookseller listings. [S]
- 1940 | Third edition, Chapman & Hall, 258 pp., green cloth, pictorial
  jacket. Bookseller listing. [S] Wellcome copy said by the brief to be the
  BMA's; provenance not seen. [U]
- 1941 | A further printing cited in one bookseller-derived excerpt. [U]
- 1942 | Aldous Huxley, The Art of Seeing, preface, lists among books on
  visual education "The Improvement of Sight by Natural Methods, by C. S.
  Price, M.B.E., D.O. (London, 1934)", with Bates's Perfect Sight Without
  Glasses (New York, 1920) and Corbett's How to Improve Your Eyes (Los
  Angeles, 1938). Several online transcriptions agree. [T]
- 1943 | Radionic Association founded. Early figures named in secondary
  histories (de la Warr, Rae, Wethered, Wilcox, Russell, Da Monte, Dower,
  Eden) do not include Price. [S]
- 1946 | Hardback copy dated 1946 offered on eBay (item 166614967379),
  180 x 120 mm. Publisher not stated in the excerpt. [S]
- 1949 | Margaret Corbett, Help Yourself to Better Sight, names "The Ferris
  School of Eye Sight Training" in London as using the Bates system. A
  different school; do not conflate with Price's. [S]
- 1950 | Scientific and Technical Congress of Radionics and Radiesthesia,
  London, 16–18 May. No Price found among names surfaced. [S]
- undated | "School of Eyesight Training" and "Radionic Therapy Centre":
  no address, date or directory entry found. [U]
- undated | Death. Not found. [U]
- undated | BMA, GMC, prosecution or press controversy. Not found. Only the
  1925 "debarred from institutions" remark bears on this. [U]
